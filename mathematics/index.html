Graph Theory

Graph theory is the study of vertices and the edges that join them. A graph is written \( G = (V, E) \). From that one definition come walks, cycles, colourings, and the questions that organise most of combinatorics.

Related notes:

  • What is a graph? Vertices, edges, simple graphs and multigraphs.
  • Hamiltonian and Eulerian graphs. Cycles through every vertex, and tours through every edge.
  • Uniquely Hamiltonian graphs. Graphs with exactly one Hamiltonian cycle, and Sheehan’s conjecture.
